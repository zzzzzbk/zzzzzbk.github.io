<p>Inspired by how an octopus wraps and entangles prey, I study a multi-arm soft gripper that grasps not by pinching but by tangling around objects. Using high-fidelity simulation, I analyze how many slender, compliant tentacles interact to produce robust, shape-agnostic grasps — even on irregular objects that rigid grippers struggle with.</p>

<h2>Highlights</h2>
<ul class="detail-list">
  <li>Used continuum simulation to reveal the dynamic entanglement, contact mechanics, and grasp topology of a multi-arm soft gripper.</li>
  <li>Characterized how deterministic origami-based tentacles produce reliable grasps through <em>stochastic</em> entanglement.</li>
  <li>Connected simulation insight to physical gripper designs for robust robotic grasping.</li>
</ul>
