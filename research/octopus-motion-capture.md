<p>Studying how a real octopus moves requires seeing inside a cloud of soft, deforming, self-occluding arms — something optical motion capture cannot do underwater. I develop a computer-vision pipeline that reconstructs the full 3D kinematics of octopus arms from data acquired by an underwater ultrasound-based motion-capture system, making the animal's tangled behavior measurable.</p>

<h2>Highlights</h2>
<ul class="detail-list">
  <li>Applied computer-vision and point-cloud reconstruction algorithms to ultrasound motion-capture data.</li>
  <li>Recovered 3D arm kinematics during complex, tangled octopus behavior that defeats optical tracking.</li>
  <li>Produced quantitative motion data that grounds the biomechanics and soft-robotics models in real animal movement.</li>
</ul>
