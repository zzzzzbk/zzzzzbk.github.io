  <h1>Stochastic Dynamics of Cluttered Locomotion</h1>

<p>Cockroaches barrel through cluttered terrain with a speed and robustness that seems to defy careful planning. Inspired by this, I built a physics-based numerical model from scratch to ask a counterintuitive question: can <em>randomness</em> help a body get through a field of obstacles? The answer turns out to be yes — body vibrations statistically ease traversal.</p>

<h2>Highlights</h2>
<ul class="detail-list">
  <li>Developed a physics-based simulation from the ground up — contact, collision detection, and constraints — for ellipse-shaped bodies navigating obstacle-dense environments.</li>
  <li>Applied Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) methods to model the stochastic dynamics of traversal.</li>
  <li>Showed that random body vibrations facilitate obstacle traversal, and that a potential-energy-landscape view helps predict it.</li>
</ul>
