This ongoing work investigates active stiffening in octopus arms during reaching. The central question is how muscle activity interacts with passive fibrous tissue to change an arm's mechanical response. I develop computational models to connect deformation and tissue mechanics to the physical basis of motor control.

The broader engineering interest is in compliant systems whose material and structural properties can support control. A manuscript is being prepared for submission; this page provides a high-level overview of unpublished work.
