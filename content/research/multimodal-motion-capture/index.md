Reconstructing a highly deformable animal is difficult when its arms overlap and occlude one another. I designed a multi-camera underwater imaging system and developed pipelines to recover octopus geometry and motion.

This ongoing research investigates ultrasound as a complementary sensing modality and methods for integrating optical and ultrasound measurements at the point-cloud level. The goal is to connect observations of living systems with geometric and physical representations. Findings are unpublished; this page intentionally stays at the level of the research question and approach.
