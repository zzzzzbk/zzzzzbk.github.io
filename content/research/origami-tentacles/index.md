A gripper can use many compliant contacts to secure an object without prescribing every contact in advance. This work studies how locally programmed origami deformation produces collective entanglement when several tendon-driven tentacles operate together.

My focus is on modeling and simulation: relating origami mechanics to Cosserat-rod representations and investigating the role of morphology and passive mechanics in grasping.

Published in Advanced Science (2026), e76810. Alec Boron, Bokun Zheng, and Ziyang Zhou contributed equally. DOI: 10.1002/advs.76810.
