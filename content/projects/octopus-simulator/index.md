I developed a full-body octopus simulator using muscle-actuated Cosserat-rod models for all eight arms. I then baked the simulated deformation into Blender to create underwater motion and whole-body animation.

The project connects numerical mechanics with a visual workflow, making complex deformable motion easier to inspect and communicate.
