I processed snake motion-capture data to reconstruct kinematics with more than 1,200 degrees of freedom. I applied model predictive control to estimate control inputs associated with muscle actuation and environmental interaction.

This project explores how observed motion can inform an estimate of the forces and actuation that produce it.
