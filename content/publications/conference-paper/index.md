Alec Boron*, Bokun Zheng*, Ziyang Zhou*, Noel Naughton, Suyi Li

*Equal contribution

Simple tendon pulls create complex collective grasping. I model how origami-inspired tentacles deform, make contact, and entangle—connecting individual tentacle design to the behavior of a multi-tentacle gripper.
